Our Heritage

A Fifteen-Year Legacy of Educational Transformation

Founded in 2011 with a bold vision to redefine primary and secondary education in Minembwe, MINEMBWE SCHOOL began as a modest academy of 120 students and 8 dedicated educators. Guided by a core conviction that every child possesses innate genius that blooms in the right environment, the institution swiftly distinguished itself through unmatched scholastic rigor and moral discipline.

Over the past 15 years, Minembwe School has expanded into an internationally benchmarked center of learning, serving more than 1,400 students across Early Childhood, Primary, and Secondary sections. Our campus features modern science facilities, digital learning suites, an expansive sports complex, and an innovative arts pavilion.

2011 — The Foundation

Inception of Minembwe School with modern nursery and early primary classrooms focused on literacy and arithmetic.

2016 — Campus Expansion & Science Complex

Inauguration of modern chemistry, biology, and physics laboratories alongside international curriculum integration.

2021 — STEM & Robotics Innovation Wing

Launch of our 100-seat coding academy and automated robotics workshop for young engineers.

2026 & Beyond — Vision 2030 Leadership

Ranked consistently among the nation's premier schools, preparing graduates for top global universities and societal impact.

Pedagogical Vision

Our Educational Philosophy: Developing The Whole Child

At MINEMBWE SCHOOL, we reject rote memorization. We champion Inquiry-Based Learning that engages children as active researchers of their world.

"Education is not the filling of a pail, but the lighting of a fire."

Our pedagogical framework balances three interconnected dimensions:

  • Cognitive Mastery: Deep conceptual understanding, rigorous logic, and mastery of STEM, humanities, and linguistic arts.
  • Emotional & Social Intelligence: Collaborative teamwork, emotional resilience, conflict resolution, and self-efficacy.
  • Physical & Aesthetic Well-being: Daily athletic vitality, visual arts, music, and mindfulness in a serene environment.
