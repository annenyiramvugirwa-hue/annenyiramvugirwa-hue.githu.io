Answers & Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Find fast answers to common questions about admissions, school bus transportation, curriculum, and boarding facilities.

What are the admission age cut-offs for Nursery and Primary 1?

Children entering Nursery 1 (Baby Class) should be at least 3 years old by September. For Primary 1 (Grade 1), children should have attained 6 years of age or successfully completed 3 years of accredited early childhood education.

Do you offer school bus transportation for students?

Yes! MINEMBWE SCHOOL maintains a fleet of modern, air-conditioned school buses fitted with GPS tracking, seat belts, and trained chaperones. Our bus routes cover major sectors across Kigali including Gacuriro, Nyarutarama, Kacyiru, Kimironko, Remera, Kicukiro, and Kibagabaga.

Is Minembwe School a day school or boarding school?

We operate primarily as a premier day school with extended after-school study, club programs, and care until 5:30 PM. For Senior Secondary students (Grades 10 - 12), we offer optional modern residential boarding facilities designed to provide focused academic preparation.

What is the school fee payment structure and schedule?

School fees are structured per term (3 terms per academic year). Fees cover tuition, textbooks, digital learning platforms, science and computer lab access, and routine sports activities. Flexible termly installment arrangements are available upon consultation with the Finance Office.

How does the school support students moving from other countries or curricula?

We have extensive experience welcoming international students and expatriate families. Our Academic Transition Team evaluates previous curricula and provides bridging classes in French, English, and local languages to ensure a smooth, confident integration.
